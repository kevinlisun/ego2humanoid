### Tactile-grounded dexterous grasp (owner request 2026-11-12): P (perception) and H (hand) tracks

Problem: without tactile the retargeted grasp is open-loop; the Dex3 (3-finger) hand also has a large embodiment gap to
the 5-finger human hand. Two tracks, developed in parallel and joined in track J.

**Track P — FAH + EgoTac + Physics-based Contact Refinement (PCR).**
FAH gives MANO pose (wrist/finger, penetration-prone, no force); EgoTac gives per-vertex contact probability and a
normalised force (not Newtons, E.7). PCR turns both into a physically consistent hand–object trajectory: optimise
MANO pose/translation per frame in a short window so that (a) vertices with EgoTac contact `p>τ` touch the object
surface (signed distance ≈ 0), (b) vertices with `p<τ` do not penetrate, (c) pose stays close to FAH (data term) and
smooth in time, (d) object follows a physics-feasible path (MuJoCo/CoACD proxy: gravity, friction cone, the contact
set must support the object weight, with force magnitudes scaled by one calibrated gain per clip). Output replaces
the grasp-time portion of the FAH hand and adds a refined contact set. Prior art to reuse for the loss design:
ContactOpt, DexYCB/HOI refinement, SPIDER's contact-guided sim optimisation (already in `third_party/spider`).

**Track H — five-finger tactile hand.** Candidate: **Sharpa Wave** (22 DoF, compliant fingertip pads, MJCF in
`mujoco_menagerie/sharpa_wave` and `sharpa-robotics/sharpa-urdf-usd-xml`; the owner wrote "shapa", assumed the same
hand — confirm in Q9). Fallbacks: Shadow Hand (24 DoF, menagerie MJCF, add touch sites ourselves), Inspire RH56
(5-finger, 6 DoF, simple), Allegro/LEAP (4-finger, not human-like). Selection criterion: MJCF available, human-like
kinematics (so MANO→hand retarget works), contact/touch readout possible in MuJoCo. Mounted on the G1 wrist
(replacing Dex3) or as a floating hand + arm for ablations; the arm IK stays.
